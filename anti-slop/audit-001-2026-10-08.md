# antislop audit 001 (2026-10-08)

- **Scope:** Catera native apps on branch `v2`, HEAD `a851bb6`: customer app (`apps/customer`), Catera Dapur (`apps/caterer`), shared `packages/mobile-ui` and `packages/design-tokens`.
- **Mode:** AFTER (session override). Read-only audit; nothing in the codebase was changed. Fix only the numbers you approve.
- **Rules:** antislop core 3.2.20 (R-01..R-38, C-1..C-5, Delivery Gate), antislop-ui (incl. App & Dashboard), antislop-layoutmobile translated to React Native (web-only items such as `100vh`, `clamp()`, CSS breakpoints and `:hover` do not apply; their RN equivalents were checked instead).
- **Evidence:** source at HEAD; screenshots `output/native-review/p1/final/*.png` (taken before the final fix wave, so the 7 removed titles, the share clamp, the 48dp route actions, the trial card, the change-day empty message and the "masih berjalan" colour were judged from code); WCAG ratios computed with node.
- **Direction (R-37):** DESIGN.md, PRODUCT.md, `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-08-native-uiux-motion-design.md`.

**Design Read:** Reading this as: two Indonesian-first native phone apps, a warm food-led subscription-catering customer app and a calm, denser kitchen operations app (Dapur), in Catera's forest/sunrise/cream language with Plus Jakarta Sans, food photography as the focal material and the mascot as occasional warmth.

**Dials** (derived; DESIGN.md declares none, see #29):

| App | ENERGY | RHYTHM | MOTION | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Customer | 2 | 2 | 2 | "generous and warm" customer screens, photo-led discovery; motion = story beats plus press/selection micro-feedback (spec interview #2) |
| Dapur | 1 | 1 | 1 (+ press micro-feedback) | "operations are restrained and denser"; "utility + small wins"; operational rows and money totals stay still |

Priority mapping: HIGH = Hard Gate rule, MEDIUM = Purpose-Gate rule, LOW = Quality Lock (Craftsmanship C-x and skill-checklist items are mapped to the nearest tier and say so).

## Findings

### HIGH

**1. HIGH · Shared · R-38 / C-5**
Synthetic demo data is never labelled in either native app: generated food photos, invented caterers ("Dapur Senja", "Hijau Kitchen"), "Kapasitas 100 porsi" and seeded reviews read as real listings.
Evidence: `packages/mobile-core/src/provider.tsx:32,85` exposes `demo`, but native uses it only for "Bayar (demo)" (`apps/customer/src/buy/PaymentScreen.tsx:179,238`) and the dev login caption; web shows a ribbon (`apps/web/src/components/application.tsx:337-345`); c8, c9, c14, d5, d6 show no label. DESIGN.md:372 requires "explicitly labeled demo listings".
Fix: render a persistent "Demo · data sintetis" strip in both app shells whenever `demo` is true.

**2. HIGH · Customer · C-5 (mapped to the R-38 honesty tier)**
The per-meal price is labelled "Per porsi" on package detail and has no unit on catalog cards, so a lunch+dinner package shows half its real per-portion price.
Evidence: `discover/PackageDetail.tsx:71-73` and `discover/PackageCard.tsx:39-41` show `perMealPrice()` (= price / 2 for `meal: "both"`, `packages/domain/src/index.ts:493-494`); `today/EmptyHome.tsx:43` shows the full price "per hari"; web labels the same number "/ sekali makan · 2 kali makan / hari" (`apps/web/src/components/marketplace.tsx:218-221`). PRODUCT.md rule 1: one portion of a combined offer covers both meals.
Fix: label per-meal prices "/ sekali makan" (plus "2 kali makan / hari" for combined offers) on card and detail, and align EmptyHome.

**3. HIGH · Dapur · R-02**
Two user-visible em dashes (U+2014) remain.
Evidence: `packages/domain/src/kitchen.ts:188` menu share header ("Menu {katering} <em dash> {paket}"), sent to WhatsApp by "Bagikan menu" (`apps/caterer/src/menu/MenuWeek.tsx:127`) and pinned by `tests/kitchen.test.ts:244`; `apps/caterer/src/import/ImportAssistant.tsx:232` shows an em dash for a row with no package (next to "? hari lagi").
Fix: use "Menu Dapur Senja · Ayam Panggang Harian"; replace the placeholders with "Paket belum dipilih" and "sisa hari belum diisi".

**4. HIGH · Dapur · R-38**
"Yang dimasak" lists undecided menu slots ("Lauk ×2 · 2", "Nasi ×1 · 1", "Sayur ×1 · 1") as if they were dishes, mixed with real dishes and without saying the menu is not filled.
Evidence: `packages/domain/src/kitchen.ts:99-101` (category fallback rows), `apps/caterer/src/today/SessionCard.tsx:87-92`; screenshot d2.
Fix: group by package and label the fallback "Menu belum diisi: 2 lauk, 1 nasi, 1 sayur" with a link to Menu.

**5. HIGH · Shared · R-03**
The shared `Chip` and `Segmented` tap targets are 40dp tall (antislop floor 44, DESIGN.md 48).
Evidence: `packages/mobile-ui/src/components.tsx:333,344`; used for Dapur Pelanggan filters (`customers/CustomerList.tsx:37`, d3), Menu package chips (`menu/MenuWeek.tsx:161`, d4), Hari ini/Besok (`today/TodayScreen.tsx:70`, d1, d2), customer language and change-day mode (`account/Akun.tsx:43`, `schedule/ChangeDaySheet.tsx:147`, c7, c13). `discover/FilterChip.tsx:4` already works around it.
Fix: raise chip and segment `minHeight` to 48 (or give the segment a hitSlop covering the 4px track padding).

**6. HIGH · Dapur · R-27**
Pelanggan has no loading state: while the list loads the chips read "Aktif · 0 / Segera berakhir · 0 / Selesai · 0", a false count, and the error has no retry.
Evidence: `apps/caterer/src/customers/CustomerList.tsx:18-43`.
Fix: show "Memuat pelanggan…" and hide counts until `list.data` exists; add "Coba lagi" to the error.

**7. HIGH · Dapur · R-27**
Menu and Usaha have neither loading nor error states for their operations read.
Evidence: `apps/caterer/src/menu/MenuWeek.tsx:131-212` (while `ops` loads: title, no chips, no days, but "Salin minggu lalu" and "Bagikan menu" are active); `apps/caterer/src/business/UsahaScreen.tsx:49-75` (a failed or pending read leaves an empty "Paket" card with only "+ Paket baru").
Fix: "Memuat…" until data, an error line with "Coba lagi", and disable Menu actions until a week is loaded.

**8. HIGH · Dapur · R-27**
Dapur error states name the failure but offer no next action (and two render it as a muted caption that looks like loading text); there is no pull-to-refresh anywhere.
Evidence: `today/TodayScreen.tsx:88`, `business/UangScreen.tsx:14`, `business/AktifkanScreen.tsx:41`, `customers/CustomerDetail.tsx:21`, `customers/CustomerList.tsx:40`. The customer app pairs every load error with "Coba lagi".
Fix: add a "Coba lagi" button calling `reload()` and use `colors.danger` for the message.

**9. HIGH · Dapur · R-27**
A font-load failure leaves Dapur on a blank screen forever.
Evidence: `apps/caterer/app/_layout.tsx:68-69` ignores the `useFonts` error; the customer app handles it (`apps/customer/app/_layout.tsx:71-76`).
Fix: mirror the customer fallback message.

**10. HIGH · Customer · R-27**
The signed-out / no-package Beranda loads three catalog cards with no loading or error state; on a slow or failed read the food section silently disappears.
Evidence: `apps/customer/src/today/EmptyHome.tsx:11-47`.
Fix: show a short loading line while pending and, on error, a line with "Coba lagi" (the "Jelajah paket" button can stay).

**11. HIGH · Shared · R-32 (native translation: TalkBack / VoiceOver operability)**
Screen titles and section headings are not exposed as headers, so screen-reader users cannot jump by heading; the shared `Text` cannot even accept an accessibility role.
Evidence: `packages/mobile-ui/src/components.tsx:34-54` (Text props: variant, style, children, testID, numberOfLines only), `AppHeader.tsx:25`; only `today/Plate.tsx:155` and `schedule/ChangeDaySheet.tsx:121` set `accessibilityRole="header"`. Also: the Sheet scrim has a label but no role (`components.tsx:247`), and shared labels "Tutup", "Kurangi …", "Tambah …" are hard-coded Indonesian under the English locale (`components.tsx:210,222,247`).
Fix: give `title`/`heading` variants `accessibilityRole="header"`, pass through accessibility props, add `accessibilityRole="button"` to the scrim and take the labels as translated props.

**12. HIGH · Customer · R-25 · direction conflict: owner decides**

**13. HIGH · Customer · R-25 (non-text, WCAG 1.4.11) · known, deferred in Phase 1 · direction conflict: owner decides**

Fix: owner keeps it (record the override) or the icon and ring switch to `sunriseInk` (5.73:1 on scheduled).

**14. HIGH · Shared · R-03 (code-verified, not device-tested)**
The shared `Screen` has neither the 760 width cap nor the keyboard avoidance DESIGN.md:240 documents; the customer app allows landscape and iPad, where every card and photo stretches to full width, and no screen avoids the keyboard on iOS.
Evidence: `packages/mobile-ui/src/components.tsx:257-272`; `apps/customer/app.config.ts:12,16` (`orientation: "default"`, `supportsTablet: true`); no `KeyboardAvoidingView` / `automaticallyAdjustKeyboardInsets` in any app source (e.g. review text field at `today/Beranda.tsx:244`).

**15. HIGH · Dapur · R-17**

### MEDIUM

**17. MEDIUM · Shared · R-19**
Press micro-feedback, the declared motion contract for every control, is missing on several controls and on almost every tappable row.
Evidence: plain `Pressable` with no `PressableScale`/haptic: `today/Plate.tsx:38-48` (SunriseButton, i.e. "Sudah sampai" and "Perpanjang"), `discover/FilterChip.tsx:15`, Plate reactions `:197`, Stepper buttons `components.tsx:208-228`, Beranda stars `:232`, Jadwal month arrows `schedule/Jadwal.tsx:78-96`, Claim back `claim/ClaimScreen.tsx:284`. Rows with no pressed state on Android: UpcomingRows, MenuDueRows, Jadwal MealRow, MonthGrid cells, PackageCard, EmptyHome cards, Dapur Usaha Row, CustomerList, MenuWeek day cards (only `account/Row.tsx:47` and `account/Notifications.tsx:107` dim). DESIGN.md:353.
Fix: route button-like controls through `PressableScale`; give rows a pressed opacity or `android_ripple`.

**18. MEDIUM · Shared · R-19**
Bottom sheets always slide in, ignoring reduced motion on iOS (Android's "Remove animations" happens to suppress it).
Evidence: `packages/mobile-ui/src/components.tsx:246` (`animationType="slide"` unconditionally); DESIGN.md:357 "replaces scale and movement with opacity-only feedback or no motion".
Fix: `animationType={reduced ? "fade" : "slide"}` via `useReduced()`.

**19. MEDIUM · Shared · R-04**
The icon set is Expo's default Ionicons, mixed filled (customer tab bar) and outline (Dapur tabs, all content icons), with no written reason for the library or the split.
Evidence: `apps/customer/app/(tabs)/_layout.tsx:8-13` (filled), `apps/caterer/app/(tabs)/_layout.tsx:8-13` and content icons (outline); DESIGN.md:321 only says "consistent SVG interface icons".
Fix: write one line in DESIGN.md naming the set and the filled/outline rule, or align the tab bars.
