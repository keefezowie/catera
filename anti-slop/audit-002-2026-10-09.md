- **Mode:** after (session override). This is the Delivery Gate record for Phase A of the native visual identity (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-09-native-visual-identity-design.md`, sections 3.1, 3.2 and 10).

- **Design Read:** reading this as a food-led customer meal-subscription app and a restrained kitchen-operations app, for Indonesian households and home caterers, in a warm forest, cream and charcoal language that now also has a neutral charcoal dark theme. Dials as recorded in DESIGN.md: customer ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2; Dapur ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1 (plus press micro-feedback). Phase A adds no new composition and no motion (a theme change is not animated), so the dials are unchanged.
