## Proposals

### A. One loading vocabulary (findings 1 to 4, 16)

Three tiers, each with one job (research section 1):

1. **Skeleton** where the shape is known. A `Skeleton` primitive in `packages/mobile-ui` (rounded blocks in the theme `sage`, a slow opacity pulse between 0.55 and 1, transform and opacity only, static under reduced motion) and one skeleton per data screen that matches the loaded layout's heights: Beranda (hero card, other-meal row, three list rows), Jelajah rows, plan detail, Akun lists, Dapur Hari ini session card, Pelanggan rows, Menu week. Native chrome, the mood header and its toggle render at once. Skeleton to content is one `FadeSwap` of the section (`content` 220ms).
2. **Mascot** where nothing can be shown yet. Restore `MascotLoading` natively from the surviving layers and `packages/brand/motion.ts` (the deleted component in `d33069f0^` is the starting point), as a shared `packages/mobile-ui` component so Dapur gets it too. Keep the approved rules: 300ms reveal delay, no minimum display time, static under reduced motion, paused when backgrounded, a task line under it. Uses: the launch gate when there is truly nothing to show, sign-in, "Membaca daftar pelanggan…" in the import assistant, "Memeriksa pembayaran…" on Bayar.
3. **Inline** progress on the control or object: the existing busy button, a spinner over a photo tile while it uploads, the platform's pull-to-refresh on Beranda, Jadwal, Hari ini and Pelanggan.

Rule for DESIGN.md: never a bare full-screen `ActivityIndicator`, never two loaders in sequence for one screen.

### B. One launch sequence (findings 3 to 6)

- **Hold the native splash until the first screen can paint:** `SplashScreen.preventAutoHideAsync()` at module load; hide once fonts, the stored theme and the cached session are read (local reads, tens of ms), never waiting on the network.
- **Paint from cache first:** the session and the last `home:customer` read are already on disk; show them at once with a quiet "Memperbarui…" line, and swap in fresh data with `FadeSwap`. A first-ever launch shows the Beranda skeleton under a live header. Only a first launch that cannot even reach `me()` shows the mascot gate, then the existing "Belum bisa terhubung" error.
- **Splash art (Decision B):** the theme canvas (light `#FDFAF3`, dark `#151514`) with a small transparent mark (the `logo-forest`/`logo-light` pair, or the mascot) instead of the opaque icon tile; `splash.dark` in both configs; Android adaptive icon from `adaptive-foreground.png` on forest.
- **Optional animated hand-off** (research section 2, Pinterest): after the native splash hides, the same mark on the same canvas plays one short beat (the mascot's existing loop once, about 600ms) while the first screen mounts underneath, then fades. Never delays data; skipped under reduced motion and on warm starts.

### C. Pengaturan in both apps (findings 7, 8)

- New pushed screen **Pengaturan** in the account tab (customer `/(akun)/pengaturan`, Dapur `/(usaha)/pengaturan`), reached from a trailing gear `HeaderIconButton` on the tab root (iOS) and a "Pengaturan" row at the end of Akun and Usaha (both platforms).
- Inset grouped rows with their current value: **Notifikasi** (a native `Switch` that reflects the phone's real permission and the device registration, read on focus; turning it off unregisters the device), **Bahasa** ("Indonesia", opens a small form sheet with two options), **Tampilan** ("Sistem", same with three options), then **Tentang** (app version) and **Keluar** at the end. Signed-out customers reach the same screen without Notifikasi and Keluar.
- Akun keeps the person and their things; Usaha keeps the business. The inline segmented controls leave both.

### D. Action hierarchy and native sheets (finding 11)

A written ladder (research section 5), applied screen by screen:

| Weight | Container |
| --- | --- |
| A task with its own context or a cold link | Pushed page (unchanged) |
| A short task, 1 to 5 controls | Native form sheet: `presentation: "formSheet"`, `sheetAllowedDetents` (`"fitToContents"` or `[0.5, 1]`), grabber, from react-native-screens 4.26 (UISheetPresentationController on iOS, Material bottom sheet behaviour on Android) |
| Several secondary actions on one item | A short action sheet route (icon rows, destructive last) |
| An irreversible confirmation | A compact confirm sheet (icon, title, one-line consequence, two buttons) |
| A routine outcome | Toast (proposal E) |

Moves: Lapor masalah, Alamat quick add or edit, the Bantuan request forms, Bahasa and Tampilan, Dapur Tim, Dapur Laporan reply, Ubah hari, review, Dapur stop exceptions and the import row editor become form-sheet routes; a "…" on each Hari row, the Beranda hero and Dapur packages opens an action sheet; Mulai masak, Berangkat antar and discard-changes move from `Alert.alert` to confirm sheets. The custom `Sheet` retires once its last caller moves. Pages stay pages where a cold link must land (plan detail, Hari, Pilih menu, package detail). To verify in Expo Go on Android: form-sheet detents, keyboard avoidance (the custom `Sheet` handles it today), back closing the sheet, and TalkBack focus.

### E. Feedback sized to the action (findings 12 to 15)

- **Beat** for milestones: payment received, plan bought or renewed, first package published, customers imported. One drawn sequence of about 700ms: a check that draws inside a ring that grows from the hero (or the rantang lid closing), the `success` haptic on the same frame, then the facts. Plays once on the transition only (ruling D8 stays), static under reduced motion. Confetti only for a first purchase and a first published package, in palette colours.
- **Toast** for routine outcomes: a `Toast` host in `packages/mobile-ui` (one at a time, top on iOS under the bar, bottom above the tab bar on Android like a Material snackbar, auto-dismiss 3s, optional action "Urungkan" or "Lihat", announced to screen readers). Copy names the result: "Menu Selasa tersimpan", "Alamat disimpan", "Ulasan terkirim", "Paket diterbitkan".
- **Micro** on the control: the heart pop (scale 1 to 1.2 to 1 on a critically damped spring, a few sunrise-ink marks bursting out under 400ms, `select` haptic on save only) with "Disimpan · Lihat" and "Dihapus dari simpanan · Urungkan".
- **Insertions and removals** animate with Reanimated `entering`/`exiting` (fade plus 8dp rise, `content` 220ms) and `LinearTransition` for the neighbours; first loads of a list do not stagger (Decision A).

### F. Macros back on screen (finding 9)

- Customer `PackageDetail`: a "Gizi per porsi" card with four tiles (Kalori, Protein, Karbohidrat, Lemak) from `nutritionMetrics(packageNutrition(o))`, ranges shown as "450–520", the caption "Perkiraan dari katering"; hidden when the caterer filled nothing. Jelajah rows add "± 520 kkal · 32 g protein" when present.
- Dapur `PackageEditor`: an optional "Gizi per porsi" section with four number fields (and a range toggle), validated by the domain `nutritionSchema`; carried through "Salin jadi paket baru"; shown on Dapur's package view.
- Data: the demo seed already has values. Hosted packages need caterers to enter them (or the owner supplies values for the clean catalogue); Catera never estimates them.

### G. Import photo previews (finding 10)

Keep the picked `uri`; show 72dp thumbnails (radius 12, `sage` ground while decoding, `expo-image`) with a 48dp remove target and "Foto 2 dari 6" for screen readers; PDF and spreadsheets keep icon plus name; an "+ Foto" tile while under six; tap opens a full-size preview. While the assistant reads the files, the mascot with "Membaca daftar pelanggan…" (proposal A).

### H. Housekeeping (findings 16, 17)

Fix the worklets mismatch and the Dapur workspace link before any motion task; update MASCOT-MOTION.md and DESIGN.md with the Phase F gate.

## Decisions for the owner

- **Decision A, stagger and entrance (item 7 versus DESIGN.md "Native motion" and identity spec §7):** today "Never stagger a catalog, any list or any sequence" and "No entrance animation and no stagger for the calendar, lists or catalogues". Options: (1) keep the rule for data loads (a section cross-fades from its skeleton as one block) and allow motion only for elements the user's own action inserts or removes; (2) also allow a short entrance stagger (about 30ms per row, first five rows, `content` duration) on a screen's first content after its skeleton, never on refresh, search typing or tab return; (3) lift the rule.
- **Decision B, splash artwork (item 3 versus DESIGN.md "Splash", October 9):** the ruling waits for "real transparent artwork". Options: (1) accept the fifteen transparent derivatives (segmented from the opaque masters, original 1254px, not upscaled) as that artwork and ship a canvas splash with a light and dark variant; (2) keep cream until newly generated transparent masters exist; (3) draw the splash mark in code (the rantang) and export it for the splash.
- **Decision C, verification:** Expo Go never shows Catera's splash, and this machine cannot build a development client (no JDK or Android SDK). The splash change can be checked only on a machine that can build, or after a store build. Accept "configured, unverified on device" for the splash, or set up a dev build first.
- **Dials:** proposal E raises motion on the customer side within MOTION 2 (transitions and feedback). Dapur's MOTION 1 would gain the toast, the insert/remove motion and the import beat. Keep Dapur at 1 with these listed as accepted feedback (as the Phase C tick was), or move Dapur to 2.
