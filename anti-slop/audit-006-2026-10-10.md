- **Mode:** after (session override, carried from earlier Catera sessions). Nothing in this audit has been changed in code. The owner picks numbers; only those get fixed.
- **Scope:** the owner's request of 2026-10-10, made while Phase D runs: header behaviour across page transitions, Android predictive back, the header design itself, the customer Beranda for customers with several running plans, and (added mid-pass) the bottom navigation bar. ## Proposals

### Header system (findings 1 to 6)

One `ScreenHeader` anatomy for tab roots and pushed screens, replacing the two that exist today:

- **Top row, always 56dp:** back or close (pushed), or the meta line (roots), on the left; the trailing control (toggle, month arrows, area picker) on the right. Same y on every screen, so the row never moves on a tab switch or a push.
- **Large title below, in the same place on every screen** (`title`, 30/39), then screen-specific content (arc, month grid, search).
- **Collapse on scroll:** when the large title passes under the top row, a compact bar fades in (opacity only, 180ms, `selection`) with the back button and a 17pt title, on the same mood fill, and the bottom corners go square. The mood block itself scrolls away as today. This keeps back reachable everywhere (finding 3) and is the standard pattern on Mobbin ([Deepstash topic detail](https://mobbin.com/flows/444e3082-3d06-4bee-8aa2-dda012e31276), [Google Arts & Culture](https://mobbin.com/flows/4d11ed74-2720-4212-8dd1-c87801c39eac)). Built with a Reanimated scroll handler, transform and opacity only, instant under reduced motion.
- **Stable titles:** pushed screens receive their title from the link (plan name, day label) so the header is final on the first frame; loading states keep the final height with a skeleton of the right size instead of "Memuat…" in a short block.
- **Visual fixes (finding 5), for the owner to choose:** a 1dp `line` border on the white round buttons; a slightly deeper light Siang fill so the header reads as the top of the screen (contrast tests rerun for all four looks); and, on Beranda, make the day arc's sun and moon the Siang/Malam switch (48dp targets on the markers) so the separate toggle row goes and the hero rises by about one row. That last one changes an owner ruling (the toggle is the mood control), so it is a question, not a default.
- **Motion (finding 6):** pushes use one explicit animation on both platforms (candidate: `ios_from_right` in react-native-screens, where the previous screen shifts with parallax), modals `slide_from_bottom`, the story keeps `fade`. Because the top row is fixed at 56dp on every screen, the header edge no longer steps during the slide.

### Bottom nav (finding 10)

- **Recommended: `NativeTabs` from `expo-router/unstable-native-tabs`** (present in this Expo Router version, and what `building-native-ui` prescribes). Android gets the platform Material 3 bar with an active-indicator pill (`indicatorColor` from the theme, for example a forest tint on cream and its dark pair), a tuned `rippleColor`, and labels always shown; iOS gets the system tab bar, which on iOS 26 is the floating glass bar the Mobbin references show. Ionicons still work through `NativeTabs.Trigger.VectorIcon`. To verify: the 1.15 label font-scale cap and the iOS spoken labels (DESIGN.md, Tab labels) may not be settable on the native bar, so font scale 1.3 at 360dp must be rechecked; behaviour in Expo Go; both apps.
- **Fallback: keep JS tabs with a custom `tabBar`:** a floating rounded bar (radius 28, `surface`, 1dp `line` border and no shadow, so the hero stays the one raised surface under the Quiet Work Rule) with a pill behind the selected tab that slides between tabs (transform only, 180ms). Content reserves the bar's height so nothing hides under it.

### Predictive back (finding 7)

A time-boxed spike in a development build (Expo Go cannot show it), reported before anything ships:

1. Set `android.predictiveBackGestureEnabled: true`; on API 34, 35 and 36 emulators check that hardware and gesture back still reach JS (`BackHandler`, the stack pop, the Bayar "paid is final" guard, sheets).
2. Add a small native piece (config plugin or local module) that disables React Native's back callback while the root navigator cannot go back, so on the tab roots the system plays its back-to-home preview.
3. In-app peek (dragging shows the previous screen): not available from the classic react-native-screens stack. Evaluate the library's newer stack when it is stable, or a custom edge gesture with `react-native-gesture-handler` (already in `node_modules` through Expo, not a direct dependency). Likely deferred; the spike reports the cost.

### Beranda with several plans (findings 11 to 15)

Reading the screen's job as "what arrives today, and what needs me", the order becomes:

1. **Hari ini, by meal.** The headline names the meal and counts when there is more than one: "Siang ini, 2 antaran." With one delivery it keeps today's form and names the main dish by the story's rule (finding 11). When a meal has two or more deliveries the hero becomes a horizontal pager of identical hero cards with "1 dari 2", so no delivery is demoted into a different card (finding 12). References: [Keeta ongoing orders](https://mobbin.com/screens/1ef5ecf1-1b8c-4c55-8398-5d33b2ed3dd1), [Glovo orders](https://mobbin.com/screens/fbc931a3-b5ce-42a4-8755-d259bceed610), [DoorDash order card](https://mobbin.com/screens/aff3baf7-3bb3-4f49-bdac-0e2803a33c38). The other meal stays the one-row switch.
2. **Perlu kamu** (needs you): one section of compact rows, one style, each with its single action: "Pilih menu Selasa" rows, "Ikan Bumbu Kuning · sisa 2 hari · Perpanjang", "Rantang Nusantara selesai hari ini · Lihat paket penuh", the review prompt. At most one filled accent button on the screen (finding 13). Hidden when empty.
3. **Menu besok** row (unchanged).
4. **Berikutnya, by day:** one day header ("Senin 12 Okt · 3 antaran") with a row per meal and plan under it (finding 14). Reference: [HelloFresh week strip](https://mobbin.com/screens/d0473d9c-8008-4a8d-8d1c-3a64bc20967d), [Alma today's food](https://mobbin.com/screens/0ab36587-2c20-4acb-aa76-d55e0ec3983d).
5. **Paket aktif:** one row ("8 paket aktif") that opens the plan list, or a horizontal strip of small plan chips; duplicate names carry their date range (finding 15).

This needs a short spec with the owner's choices before a plan, and it overlaps Phase D's `RecapCard` on Beranda, so it starts after Phase D merges.

## Owner decisions, 2026-10-10

- **Bottom nav:** `NativeTabs` (finding 10, recommended option).
- **Platform-native chrome:** each platform follows its own guidelines rather than one shared look (the GitHub mobile app is the reference). iOS uses HIG components (system tab bar, large-title navigation bars, swipe back, form sheets); Android uses Material 3 (navigation bar with indicator pill, top app bars, predictive back). Catera's identity stays in the content layer: mood blocks on tab roots, the day arc, the lunchbox, food photos, Plus Jakarta Sans. Reviews use `apple-design` for iOS and `material-3` for Android. This replaces the custom `AppHeader` convention for pushed screens and changes the header proposal above: pushed screens get the native stack header instead of a shared `ScreenHeader`.
- **Two or more deliveries of a meal:** swipeable heroes (finding 12, option 1).
- **Arc as the Siang/Malam switch:** kept, "clear and obvious for the user without being too much". Both ends are drawn as same-size circles (filled when selected, an outline ring when not), with the meal's time or delivery count under each label. See the canvas ([Catera Beranda directions](https://claude.ai/artifact/VRTSbG3tp8j2fTja2apdDR)).
- **Whole Beranda order** (canvas `HomeFull`) and the native-chrome consequences (the tab bar stays on detail screens; detail screens drop the mood block and use the native bar): approved.
- **One typeface:** Plus Jakarta Sans stays everywhere the app draws text, including native bar titles and tab labels, at each platform's native sizes. "We're not chasing 100% native look like built-in system apps, but at least keep the native components." System-drawn UI (alerts, share sheet, keyboard, pickers) keeps the system font.
