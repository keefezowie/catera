# Copy audit 001: labels and wording (2026-10-31)

Mode: after (audit first, fix only approved numbers). Rules: humanizer + antislop core and copywriting skill. Reference: Gojek, GoPay, Grab and Shopee screens from Mobbin plus help-centre copy from GoBiz, GrabMerchant, ShopeeFood and Tokopedia ([audit-001-detail/mobbin-reference.md](audit-001-detail/mobbin-reference.md)).

Per-area tables with exact lines, suggested ID/EN rewrites and the tests that pin each string live in [audit-001-detail/](audit-001-detail/). This file is the pick list.

## Summary

- The native apps (customer and Catera Dapur) are already in good shape. They use kamu throughout, verb-first buttons, sentence case, one-line errors ending in "Coba lagi", and contain no buzzwords, emoji, chatbot residue or not-X-but-Y. `tests/copy-scan.test.ts` already guards them. Their findings are naming drift and a handful of long or stiff sentences.
- `apps/web` is where the real problems are. It reads like a translated operations console: em dashes, formal "Anda" next to kamu, "katerer", "Silakan", "telah", and an English-only tagline in the page title. `copy-scan` does not scan `apps/web/src` or `packages/backend`.
- Priority follows antislop: HIGH = hard gate (em dash, unbacked claim), MED = clear tell or inconsistency, LOW = polish.

## Owner decisions (answer these before fixing)

- **A. Dapur pronoun.** Merchant apps (GoBiz, GrabMerchant, ShopeeFood) address sellers as "Anda"; Dapur uses "kamu" and the glossary bans "Anda". Recommendation: keep kamu. Your caterers are small home kitchens, and Gojek's merchant "Anda" is corporate boilerplate.
- **B. En dash in numeric ranges** ("Sen–Jum", "5–9 Okt", "11.00–13.00", "29–30 kkal"). The humanizer bans every dash, but `copy-scan` allows unspaced range dashes and they are correct typography. Recommendation: keep unspaced range dashes and fix only the spaced or lone ones (#4, #5, #16). If you want zero dashes, approve #32 and #62 as well.
- **C. "Tim Catera biasanya memeriksa dalam 1 sampai 2 hari kerja"** (#6). Is this real policy? If not, it is an unbacked promise.
