## Consistency

- Contact number: "Nomor WhatsApp" (Lengkapi) vs "Nomor HP" (DataAkun, Claim, error text). Choose one.
- Escalation action: "Minta Catera meninjau" / "Kirim ke Catera" / "Minta bantuan" / "Kirim permintaan" for related steps in the help flow (item 4).
- "paket" is both plan and package in ID; EN switches between "plan", "plans", "package" (items 15, 16).
- "Mulai" is used as a bare CTA in onboarding and email verification, with EN "Start" and "Get started" for the same ID word.
- "kabar" vs "notifikasi" for the same feature (item 19).
- Positive: kamu voice is consistent everywhere (no "Anda"); "Coba lagi" / "Try again", "Lanjut" / "Continue", "Simpan ...", "Hapus ...", "Kata sandi" / "Password", "Belum bisa ..." errors are uniform. Sentence case is used throughout.
- Apostrophe style: curly in Payments.tsx:114 only.

## Verdict

This scope is the cleanest area I would expect in the app. The voice is plain, kamu-based, verb-first and concrete, with 1 to 2 short sentences in empty and error states and real next steps ("Cek koneksi lalu coba lagi", "Jangan bayar lagi. Statusnya muncul di sini."). There are no em dashes, not-X-but-Y constructions, buzzwords, forced triads, chatbot residue or fabricated claims. The real problems are three small clusters: inconsistent names for one thing (phone vs WhatsApp number, three labels for escalating to Catera, plan vs package), two generic "Mulai" CTAs, and a handful of over-long or hedged sentences (anti-enumeration email copy, Claim mismatch error, address notes) where the English variants also read slightly stiff. Nothing here rates HIGH; fixing items 1 to 6 would cover almost all of the value. Mobbin reference file was not present when I finished, so no adjustment was made against it.
