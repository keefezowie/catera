# Indonesian consumer app UI copy reference (for the Catera copy audit)

Collected 2026-10-31. Only strings I actually saw are listed. Each block names its source.

## Sources and caveats

- **[M] Mobbin (iOS screenshots).** Mobbin has Gojek, GoPay, Grab and Shopee. All four were captured on Indonesian accounts (Rp prices, Jakarta/Tangerang addresses) but with the app language set to **English**. So these are the official EN variants of Indonesian apps. Merchant or seller promo text inside them stays in Indonesian.
- **Not on Mobbin:** Tokopedia, Traveloka, Dana, OVO, Halodoc, GoBiz, GrabMerchant, Shopee Seller. The searches returned other apps.
- **[W] Web fallback (Indonesian).** Official help centres quote the in-app labels and messages: gojek.com/id-id/help, gofoodmerchant.co.id (GoBiz BizTips), help.shopee.co.id, help.grab.com/merchant/id-id, tokopedia.com/help. Help-centre prose is not UI. I quote only the labels and notifications these pages attribute to the app.
- **[W-s] Search snippet only.** I saw the string in a search excerpt but couldn't open the full page. Lower confidence.
- **Gap:** I found no verbatim Indonesian notification-permission primer or empty-state copy. The only notification string is Apple's system label, listed at the end.

## Patterns

1. **Pronouns: kamu for consumers, Anda for merchants.** Consumer copy uses "kamu" and the "-mu" suffix: "pesananmu", "lidahmu", "Akunmu…". It is often casual: "udah dapet", "Gak perlu repot", "tinggal buka hape aja", with "ya"/"nih"/"kok" particles ("Coba lagi nanti ya", "danamu belum terpotong kok"). Merchant help for GoBiz, ShopeeFood and GrabMerchant uses "Anda" and plain imperatives ("Jika Anda belum memiliki menu terjadwal…"). Tokopedia also uses "Anda" in some formal errors ("…tidak tersedia untuk Anda"). Catera's customer app should use kamu and Dapur should use Anda, unless an existing ruling says otherwise.

2. **Labels are short, verb first, sentence case (Indonesian).** Examples: 'Tambah', 'Tambah pesanan', 'Pesan antar', 'Konfirmasi pesanan', 'Atur stok', 'Buat jadwal', 'Simpan', 'Kirim ulang', 'Minta link baru', 'Cek sekarang', 'Batalkan Pesanan', 'Ya, Batalkan'. Labels are 1–3 words. Indonesian copy uses sentence case. Shopee and GrabMerchant title-case some labels ("Batalkan Pesanan", "Terima Pesanan Otomatis"). Status labels are passive or progressive verb forms: "Dikemas", "Dikirim", "Selesai", "Sedang Menyiapkan", "Pesanan Siap".

3. **English variants are plain, short and casual, with house-style quirks.** Gojek uses sentence case ("Place delivery order", "Need help", "Download bill"). Grab and Shopee title-case buttons ("Place Order", "Buy Again", "Continue With Google"). The English is conversational without being cute: "Chef is cooking something good", "Your food's here! 🙂", "Hang in there, they'll be there soon!". It uses contractions freely ("We'll", "you've", "food's"). English variants keep Indonesian merchant and promo text untranslated.

4. **Loanwords are used as-is.** Common ones: checkout (in ID help: "halaman checkout"), Pickup, promo, voucher, COD, Top up, chat, driver, outlet, link/login link, CS, Custom ('Bisa Custom'). Slang abbreviations show up in promos: "10 ribuan", "10rb", "s.d", "ongkir", "HP", "hape". Nobody forces a formal Indonesian replacement when the loanword is the everyday word ("driver", not "pengemudi", in Gojek consumer copy). The formal "Mitra Pengemudi" appears only in Shopee policy text.

5. **Error and failure states: name what happened, reassure about money, say what to do.** Examples:
   - GoPay: "Oof, payment failed" + "Don't worry, your GoPay is not deducted. Please try again after some time."
   - Tokopedia: "Oops Pembayaran gagal. Tapi tenang danamu belum terpotong kok."
   - Tokopedia: "Maaf, promo gagal dipasang karena ada gangguan. Silakan coba lagi!"
   - Tokopedia: "Ada gangguan di rumah Toped! … Coba lagi atau kembali nanti."

   The interjections are short and human ("Oof", "Oops", "Maaf", "nih"). Messages are one or two short sentences. Retry phrasing is "Coba lagi", "Coba lagi nanti ya", "Please try again after some time". Specific limit errors say plainly what's wrong ("Kuota promo sudah habis", "Kebanyakan minta link, nih.").

6. **Success and celebration copy is short and specific.** Celebrations name the number: "Yay! You saved 28.000 on this purchase.", "Yay, you've saved Rp62.800 🎉!", "Thanks for the Rp5.000 tip". One interjection ("Yay!", "Whoosh!") and at most one emoji.

7. **Exclamation marks and emoji are rationed.** Exclamation marks go on welcomes, celebrations and promos ("Welcome to Gojek!", "Contribute and win!", "Let's tip your driver!", Tokopedia's "Silakan coba lagi!"). Neutral states don't get one ("Restaurant's preparing your order", "Sending your order..."). Emoji appear rarely, one per string, on status or celebration lines only: ✨, 🎉, 🙂. None appear in buttons, errors or form labels.

8. **Sentence length.** Titles and status lines run 2–6 words. Helper text is one sentence, usually under ~15 words ("Avoid hassle by keeping the exact amount ready.", "Add other dishes, if you want."). Only legal/consent text and the occasional explanation dialog (Grab "multiple orders") run long.

9. **Questions do the prompting.** Rating and feedback screens ask instead of instructing: "How was the driver?", "What went great?", "Any words for your driver?", "Need anything else?", "Anything else to add?", "Need a helping hand?". Placeholders are friendly: "Say something nice...", "Tell us what happened...", "What should people call you?".

10. **What these apps avoid (useful for spotting AI-sounding Catera copy).** I saw:
    - no "Seamless", "effortless", "elevate" or "journey" in UI copy
    - no paired-adjective taglines in functional screens
    - no "Not X, but Y" constructions
    - no explanatory subtitles under every heading
    - no stacked em-dash clauses

    The one value-prop line seen is Grab's "Your everyday everything app". Reassurance is concrete ("your GoPay is not deducted", "100% goes to the driver", "Shopee will not have access to your card info") rather than abstract ("your security is our priority"). Promo copy can be playful and slangy ("go go go order!", "BELANJA HEMAT, NO DRAMA!", "Menu bikin kenyang cuma 10 ribuan"), but that register stays in promo banners and doesn't spread into system states.
