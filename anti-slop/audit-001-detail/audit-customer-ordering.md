## Consistency
- Renew: "Perpanjang" vs "Lanjutkan paket" (finding 1).
- Edit verbs: Ubah / Ganti / Pindah (finding 2).
- Deadlines: batas pilih / batas pilihan / batas waktu / batas pesan (finding 3).
- Missing-menu states: "Menu belum diisi" (dayActions:71, WeekAgenda:144, PlanDetail:273, Jadwal:22), "Menu belum dipilih" (customer yet to pick), "Menu belum diumumkan" (caterer yet to publish). Distinct meanings, fine, but "belum diisi" is used where the customer is the one to pick; "belum dipilih" is more accurate there.
- EN plan vs package; Gimana vs Bagaimana; Chat katering vs Chat {nama}.
- Worth keeping: the error pattern "Belum bisa X. Coba lagi." (about 12 uses), "Pilih menu" CTA, "Tutup" for close, "kamu" throughout, no Anda.

## Verdict
The ordering flow reads like a competent local app: short verb-first buttons (Bayar, Pilih, Coba lagi, Tutup, Nanti saja), sentence case, kamu throughout, and helper lines that state a fact ("Pilih cara bayar di halaman berikutnya.", "Ongkir sudah termasuk.", "Cuma katering yang bisa melihat jawabanmu."). Empty states are one short line plus one helper, and the push primer ("Tahu kapan makananmu jalan") is specific and human. Nothing needs an AI-tell rewrite. The worthwhile work is a small terminology pass (renew, change, deadline, plan/package in EN) and trimming about five wordy payment and menu-choice sentences. Existing tests pin several exact strings, so each change needs matching test edits (last column).
