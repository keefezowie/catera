# Copy audit: Catera Dapur (apps/caterer)

Scope: apps/caterer/app/**, apps/caterer/src/** (all strings go through `t("id","en")`, ~600 call sites read in full), app.config.ts display strings. Mobbin reference file did not exist at finish time.
Method: extracted every `t(` call (single and multi-line) plus stray literals (RESOLVE_NOTE, field placeholders), grepped for dashes, Anda/silakan/mohon, buzzwords, chatbot residue. Result: no em dashes, no "Anda", no "silakan", no "mohon", no sales words, no emoji, no exclamation spam (one "Halo!" in the staff invite message). The area is mostly clean; findings are few.

## Consistency
- Import flow named four ways (row 8). Biggest naming drift in scope.
- "Selesai" carries three meanings: Done (button), Ended (customer status), Ends (date label) (row 7).
- "Ubah" vs "Ganti": "Ubah menu", "Ubah" (row editor), "Ubah nama atau kontak dapur" vs "Ganti" (data akun toggle), "Ganti kata sandi", "Ganti foto". Pick one: Ubah for content, Ganti for replacing a credential/photo is defensible, but state it.
- Published packages: list meta says "tayang"/"draf", buttons say "Terbitkan paket"/"Paket diterbitkan". Two verbs for one state; acceptable if "tayang" is state and "terbit" is action, but unify if possible. tests/e2e/caterer-setup.spec.ts:942 also asserts a "tayang" string.
- "-mu" suffix vs "kamu": "dapur kamu" and "foto kamu" vs "dapurmu", "emailmu", "akunmu" (rows 21, 22).
- Title Case slips amid sentence case: "Lihat Pelanggan", "Buka Pengaturan", "Kembali ke Masuk", "Tim Catera" (acceptable as a name). Status words capitalised mid-sentence ("ditandai Gagal diantar", "Mulai masak dan Berangkat") are deliberate button echoes and fine.
- Kamu is used consistently; no "Anda" anywhere. "Tim Catera" capitalised in ID, "the Catera team" lowercase in EN, fine.
- Error pattern "X belum Y. Coba lagi." is used consistently in ID (good, Gojek-like). The EN counterpart is the stiff part (row 23).

## Verdict
Catera Dapur's copy is already in good shape for home caterers: direct, operational, second-person "kamu", verb-first buttons (Berangkat, Sampai, Simpan menu, Salin minggu lalu), one-line errors with "Coba lagi", and empty states that say one thing ("Pesanan baru akan muncul di sini", "Semua beres hari ini"). The kitchen vocabulary (Dimasak, Berangkat, Sampai, Gagal diantar, Daftar masak) is specific and human, not slop. There are no em dashes, no buzzwords, no chatbot residue, no fabricated stats. Real problems are a handful of en dashes in ranges (hard ban by brief, though typographically correct), a few stiff long sentences in explanatory notes (rows 5, 6, 12, 13), the unverified "1 sampai 2 hari kerja" promise, the import-flow naming drift, and stiff passive English error strings. No rewrite of the voice is needed; this is a polish pass of roughly 24 strings.
