# antislop audit 003: native mood identity, Phase B (2026-10-09)

- **Mode:** after (session override). This is the Delivery Gate record for Phase B of the native visual identity (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-09-native-visual-identity-design.md`, sections 3.3 to 7 and 10).
- **Scope:** Phase B on `v2` from `ee51047` to `38963d4`: `nativeMood` tokens and contrast test; `MoodProvider`, `MoodHeader`, `StatusBand`, `MoodToggle`, `DayArc`, the code-drawn brand objects and the `display` variant; the mood header on every screen; the Beranda hero card; Jelajah circles, meal buttons and rows; the Jadwal photo calendar; the Dapur header, date button and count card; the Dapur Menu day strip, photo prompt and preview.
- **Skills applied:** `building-native-ui`, `antislop` (core), `antislop-ui`, `antislop-layoutmobile`.
- **Design Read:** reading this as a food-led customer meal-subscription app and a restrained kitchen-operations app, for Indonesian households and home caterers, in a warm forest, cream and charcoal language with a Siang/Malam mood on the header and hero, dials customer ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2 and Dapur ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1 (plus press micro-feedback). Identity motif: the day arc and the lunchbox. The one accent: sunrise ink (`#9B4309`, and `#F5C9A6` in dark), used for the active toggle and the today ring. The spec's sticker is Phase C and is not built.

Synthetic data only; the demo roles were entered with the apps' own "Masuk sebagai ... demo" buttons.

Several checks cannot be exercised with the demo data; they are listed under "Not verified" and are not claimed as PASS.
