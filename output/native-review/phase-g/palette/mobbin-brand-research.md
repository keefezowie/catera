## Lessons for Catera

1. **Spend the brand colour in few places.** Gojek, DoorDash, Deliveroo and Glovo keep the page and the cards neutral and put the brand on the action, the selected tab and one status surface. Catera today tints the page, the header, the cards and the tab bar in near-neighbour warm tones, which is why audit-009 found 1.03 to 1.16 separations everywhere.
2. **One hue, two depths.** Starbucks (house green and action green), Deliveroo (bright teal and text teal) and Too Good To Go use one hue at two values: a deep one for text and large blocks, a brighter one for fills and icons. That gives contrast without a second hue, unlike today's forest plus sunrise plus brown.
3. **The meal card stands on its own value, not on a shadow.** Where a status or meal card matters (Starbucks star card, Grab tracker, HelloFresh order card) it is either a solid brand block on a white page or a white card with a drawn border. None relies on a 1.03:1 tint plus a shadow.
4. **Dark mode is neutral.** Glovo's dark mode is a plain near-black with the brand only where selected. Today's dark Siang brown header and dark Malam green-on-green hero are the opposite.
5. **Photos sit on neutral or on the brand, never on a fourth tone.** Grab and DoorDash put round brand buttons on photos; sweetgreen puts one lime pill on a dark photo. A failed photo should fall back to a ground that belongs to the system (the dark photo ground), not to a pale sage.
