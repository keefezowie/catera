# How much brand colour top apps spend (Phase G-a, 2026-10-13)

The owner's hypothesis: top apps spend their primary and secondary brand colours conservatively, on buttons and a few important things, so the user can tell which action matters. Canvas directions A to J spend brand colour on headers, hero cards and big blocks.

Method: Mobbin MCP `search_screens`, iOS, 9 searches, 5 or 6 screens each. Shares are estimated by eye from the previews as a fraction of the visible screen (status bar and Mobbin footer excluded), so read them as rough bands, not measurements. Photos and illustrations are counted separately from brand-colour UI. Grab and Gojek come from the round 2 research in [mobbin-brand-research.md](mobbin-brand-research.md) and are not re-searched here. Material 3 colour roles are applied from memory because the `material-3` skill is not installed.

## Rules for Catera

1. **The ground and the bars are neutral.** Page, header and tab bar use cream and its light step, never forest or sunrise as a fill behind content. Evidence: Uber Eats, Airbnb, DoorDash, Gojek and HelloFresh keep their headers on the page colour; HelloFresh's green is a status-bar strip only. In M3 terms: `surface`, `surfaceContainerLow` and `surfaceContainer` carry the screen, `primary` does not.
2. **One filled brand control per screen: the primary action.** Forest fills "Bayar", "Perpanjang", "Pilih menu" and nothing else on that screen; secondary actions are outlined or text. Evidence: sweetgreen, Blinkit, Careem and talabat each fill exactly one button; HelloFresh and Babbel go further and use near-black, which shows the CTA can be ink if forest is needed elsewhere.
3. **Selection and the active tab get a small brand mark, not a block.** Active tab icon plus label, the selected date, the selected chip. Evidence: Airbnb's Rausch tab, Teams' today circle, Amie's selected day, Starbucks' tab, Gojek's selected tab.
4. **Trackers are ink; the brand marks only "now".** The delivery line and finished stops are charcoal or forest; sunrise (or the chosen accent) marks the current stop only. Evidence: DoorDash's tracker is black with the red reserved for the moving car; Headspace's step list is neutral with one orange dot for the current step.
5. **Personality comes from food photos and the mascot, not from coloured surfaces.** Photos take 25 to 45% of the screen in Uber Eats, Airbnb and HelloFresh and carry all the warmth. Headspace keeps its UI almost colourless and lets illustrations inside cards speak. For Catera: the meal photo leads the meal card, and the bento mascot is the warmth.
6. **At most one branded moment per screen, and only where it means something.** If a screen earns a coloured block, it is one: Starbucks' star balance, Wolt+'s plan card, Headspace's featured collection. Never a coloured header plus a coloured hero plus a coloured card. For Catera this moment is the Paket selesai recap, the payment confirmation, or an empty state with the mascot.
7. **Status, warnings and prices use their own language.** Prices are bold ink, not brand (DoorDash, sweetgreen, Wolt totals); colour on a price means a discount (Uber Eats red). Warnings are a tinted neutral box with bold text (HelloFresh's beige "automatically placed", Airbnb's outlined notice) or a semantic colour with words (Revolut's red "Verify your email"). Promotions get a separate hue from the brand (Uber red, Gojek red, Grab orange). Cards separate by tone and a hairline, with shadow kept for the one lifted card (Airbnb, HelloFresh).

## Verdict on the hypothesis

**It holds for the structure of the app.** Headers, tab bars, lists, tracking, calendars, plan detail, checkout and settings spend almost no brand colour; 21 of the 30 screens are at 4% or less, and where colour appears it is the primary button, the active tab, the selected item or the current tracker stop. That is exactly the "which action matters" signal the owner describes.

**It does not hold absolutely.** Starbucks spends about a quarter of its Order screen on house-green discs and a green pickup bar, Headspace colour-codes its programmes in big blocks, Wolt gives the plan page a blue hero card, and Grab paints its food header green. In every case it is one deliberate moment that frames content (a product, a programme, a membership), not a habit repeated on every surface, and the rest of the same app stays neutral. These apps keep their personality through photography (Uber Eats, Airbnb, HelloFresh), illustration (Headspace, Wolt), type (HelloFresh's condensed headlines, Duolingo's rounded bold) and that single branded moment. The brand is recognisable without the brand colour covering the screen.

So the draft directions' pattern, a coloured mood header plus a coloured meal card on every Beranda, is the opposite of what these apps do. The fix is not a new palette; it is moving the colour off the surfaces and onto the few things that act or signal.

## Mapping to Catera's roles

| Role | Today in the drafts | After this research |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Header (Siang/Malam mood) | Filled forest or sunrise/tan band | Cream page with the large title. Time of day carried by a small mascot or icon and the copy, not a fill. |
| Meal card | Tinted or dark fill | Light cream surface, hairline edge, the food photo leads. No brand fill. |
| Primary CTA | Forest | Forest, the only filled brand element on the screen. |
| Tracker | Coloured line and markers | Charcoal or forest line and done stops; accent (sunrise or the chosen copper) on the current stop only. |
| Tab bar | Varies | Cream surface; forest icon and label on the active tab only. |
| Prices | Sometimes accent | Bold charcoal. Accent only for a real saving. |
| Warnings (Gagal diantar, cutoff) | Accent | Semantic colour with words, or a tinted neutral box with bold text, kept apart from the brand accent. |
| Mascot art | Occasional | The one branded moment per screen: empty states, Paket selesai, payment success. |
