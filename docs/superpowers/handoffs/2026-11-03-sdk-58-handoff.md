## The confirm dialog (owner asked; built on judgement while the owner slept)
- Mobbin research: over a form or sheet, top apps confirm with a centred dialog. Examples: Shopee, talabat, Snoonu, Vipps, Yami, Keeta, Temu, Amazon, Craft. Bottom-sheet confirms appear only over full pages (Chime, X, Pinterest).
- Canvas: https://claude.ai/artifact/Gx6L6HiNbHEQXPuPQvrdw9. It shows the current stacked sheets, then the new design on iOS and Android, plus the research notes.
- Every `useConfirm` question is now `ConfirmDialog`: a fading transparent modal with a centred card over a scrim.
  - iOS: an alert-sized card, everything centred, the two buttons side by side.
  - Android: a Material 3 dialog with 28 dp corners, actions at the end edge.
  - Cancel: a tap on the scrim or the hardware back.
- Unchanged: copy, icons, danger fill, button order and answer timing. The action sheet (`/aksi`) stays a bottom sheet.
- **Owner review wanted:** the draft-first rule was followed (canvas before code), but the code went in before an owner OK. If the owner prefers the old sheet, revert `70564d6d` and the ConfirmDialog half of `7be32a24`.

## Known and accepted
- **Dev-only Android error when swiping away a sheet with unsaved text.** Swiping down an address or other form with typed text shows the red LogBox error "removed natively but didn't get removed from JS state". Android's bottom sheet can't hold a swipe, so the guard lets it through and keeps the draft, and router 58 reports it. The app state stays correct, and production builds show nothing. This is documented in `useUnsavedGuard`.
- **Things to expect when trying the apps in Expo Go:**
  - It doesn't support Sign in with Apple, universal links, remote push, or the custom icon and splash.
  - On Android, opening both apps in one Expo Go process causes ExpoCrypto errors. Force-stop Expo Go between the two apps.
  - The customer Metro needs `EXPO_PUBLIC_API_URL=http://10.0.2.2:3000` on the emulator: `.env.local` points at an old ngrok host.
