Catera apps: UX audit design drafts

Drafts for review before any code. Each pair shows what the emulator shows today (13 Nov 2026) next to the proposed change. Wireframe fidelity: real copy, approved palette, placeholder icons. Artwork and final spacing follow DESIGN.md at build time.

1. Customer · Beli: the map pin is a step, not an error

Today the first thing a buyer with an older address sees is a red error, a dash for the total and a dead Bayar button, before they have done anything wrong. The pin becomes part of the address row, the price shows straight away, and the button says what to do next.

2. Customer · Jadwal and Akun before sign-in

Two of four tabs are a sentence and a button on a blank page. A guest should see what they would get and have a second way forward.

3. Customer · Bayar: one main action

The pay page has two full-width forest buttons ("Bayar" at the top, "Sudah bayar? Cek status" at the bottom) and a large blank middle. The order summary is missing at the moment of paying.

4. Dapur · Menu opens on the next day you can still fill

The Menu tab opens on today, which is locked, so the owner lands on "Sudah tidak bisa diubah" and two disabled buttons. Filling menus is the kitchen's main weekly job; the tab should start where work is possible and show how much is left.

5. Dapur · Pelanggan: find people and act on renewals

Every row says "Berakhir hari ini" under the Aktif chip, with no search and no action until the detail page. Renewal is the kitchen's revenue moment, so it moves onto the list.

6. Dapur · Hari ini: give the cooking list the screen

The summary card, the shopping link and the pinned "Mulai masak" button leave room for barely one row of the cooking list, which is the reason the screen exists.
