# Layout research: food, delivery and meal-subscription apps (Mobbin, iOS)

Date: 2026-10-22. Scope: LAYOUT only (sizing, spacing, density, hierarchy, placement). Colour, brand and copy are out of scope.
Purpose: give the Catera customer app redesign concrete numbers to build against.

## 1. Method and coverage

- Tool: Mobbin `search_screens` (platform ios, mode deep), 36 calls, 6 results each. Every cited screen was returned as an image and examined. `search_flows` was not used.
- Measuring: Mobbin previews are 299 px wide for a 390 pt frame (1 preview px = 1.30 pt). The usable screen height in the preview is about 648 px = 845 pt. All numbers below are measured by eye on those previews and converted to pt. Treat them as +/-2 to 3 pt (+/-4 on text sizes, which are inferred from glyph height). They are good for ratios and ranges, not for pixel-perfect copying.
- Apps covered (11): Uber Eats, DoorDash, Wolt, Deliveroo, Gojek (GoFood), Grab (Food), Swiggy, Zomato, HelloFresh, Instacart, Too Good To Go.
- Pt conventions used: "margin" = outer side margin; "gap" = vertical space between sections; "row" = list row height; "viewport" = the 845 pt of usable screen.

Coverage gaps (be honest about them):

- No Factor, CookUnity or ShopeeFood food-vertical screens on Mobbin (same as earlier rounds). HelloFresh is the only true meal-subscription app and it is the most relevant one; its schedule/delivery screens are the closest analogue to Catera Beranda and Jadwal.
- No real phone-OTP login screens were captured in this round. For login I only have form-page layouts (Uber Eats "Name", Wolt "Profile details", Wolt "Name"). The login mapping in section 4 is therefore an inference from those forms.
- Zomato and Swiggy: home, menu and cart only (no account). Grab: home and merchant only. Deliveroo: home, menu, basket, checkout (no account/orders). Gojek: home, GoFood, activity, profile (no checkout). Too Good To Go: home, detail, browse, profile (no orders list).
- Mobbin shows mostly US/EU/India builds. Gojek and Grab are the only Indonesian-market references; both are dense and promo-heavy, so use them for patterns of density and chrome, not as a layout target.

## 3. Patterns

Numbers are pt on a 390 pt frame, derived from the screens above. "n of 11" counts apps where the screen type showed the pattern.

### 3.1 Consensus (most apps do this)

1. **16 pt outer margin everywhere.** 11 of 11 apps use a 16 pt side margin for text, cards and docked buttons (Gojek, Grab, Wolt, Instacart, TGTG, Uber Eats, DoorDash, HelloFresh, Deliveroo, Swiggy, Zomato). Photos and hero banners are either full-bleed or inset to the same 16 pt; nothing uses 20+ except illustrations. Inner card padding is 12-16 pt.
2. **Section heading 19-20 pt bold with a trailing "see all" affordance.** Uber Eats, DoorDash, Wolt, Deliveroo, TGTG, Instacart, Gojek (feeds) and HelloFresh all put a heading of 18-22 pt bold at the left and a 14 pt link or 30-34 pt arrow/pill at the right edge ("See all", "Show all", a circular arrow, a teal arrow). Heading to content is 12-16 pt. Between sections: 28-40 pt of whitespace, usually reinforced by a hairline or an 8 pt gray band. Secondary sub-heading is 13-14 pt gray under the heading.
3. **Horizontal rails use a "peek" and a 60-82% card width.** Card widths: Uber Eats 232, Wolt 254, Deliveroo 276, TGTG 322, HelloFresh 336 (main) and 175 (tiles), DoorDash 165 (dish) and 358 (stores), Swiggy 110, Gojek 173. The next card always shows 12-20 pt of itself with 8-12 pt gaps. Vertical lists are reserved for the thing the user came for (menu items, orders, settings); promos and recommendations are horizontal.
4. **Image-led cards: 16:9 to 2:1 for venues, 1:1 for dishes.** Venue photos 1.8:1 to 2.15:1 (Uber 1.85, Wolt 2.0, Deliveroo 2.0, TGTG 2.15, Gojek feed 2.15, Grab ad 2.4, DoorDash full-width 1.4, HelloFresh 1.7). Dish tiles are square at 165-175 pt (DoorDash, Swiggy, Grab, Gojek, HelloFresh tiles) with radius 10-20. The photo is roughly 55-65% of the card height; text below is 3 lines (name 15-17 pt bold, meta 13-14 pt gray, status line 13-14 pt).
5. **Menu / item list rows: text left, photo right, 116-154 pt tall.** Photo 100-135 pt square (Uber 103x98, Deliveroo 100, Wolt 100x66, DoorDash 135x130, Gojek 133, Zomato 152). Title 16-17 pt semibold, description 13-14 pt gray clamped to 2 lines, price 14-15 pt. The add control is a 28-36 pt circle ("+") half-overlapping the photo corner (Uber, Deliveroo, DoorDash, Swiggy, Grab), or an outlined pill 76-148 pt wide straddling the photo's bottom edge (Swiggy, Zomato, Gojek). Rows split by a hairline inset 16 pt (or none, using card borders). Visible: 4-5 rows per viewport in the dense apps (Uber, Wolt, Deliveroo), 2-3 in the photo-heavy ones (Gojek, Zomato, DoorDash).
6. **Type scale is small: 5 sizes per screen.** Typical: 28-32 (page title, only on tab roots without a hero), 20 (section), 16-17 (row title, buttons, body-primary), 14 (body secondary, meta), 12-13 (captions, caps labels). Gojek, Uber Eats and Wolt each use 4-5 sizes total on a screen; none uses more than 6. Row primary text is 16-17 pt, not 14; this is the single most visible difference from "small-text" UIs.
7. **Search + filter chips pattern:** search pill 44 pt tall (full width, radius 22 or 12, fill gray or white + 1 px border; 50-57 pt in Grab/Swiggy where it is the hero control), 16 pt below it a chip row 32-36 pt tall, 8 pt gap, horizontally scrolling, selected = filled dark or brand. Used by Uber, DoorDash, Instacart, Deliveroo, Zomato, Grab, TGTG, Swiggy.
8. **Sticky bottom CTA, full width, 47-53 pt tall.** Uber black 52 (radius 8), DoorDash red pill 47, Deliveroo teal 47 (radius 8), Instacart green pill 53 with an amount chip inside, Wolt blue 53 (radius 12), Grab green pill 52, Swiggy green 50, HelloFresh black 47, Gojek green pill 47 (two side by side in order summary). 16 pt side margin, sits above the home indicator, never inline. Detail pages put price left and CTA right (TGTG 164x48 in a 77 pt bar; Swiggy 160x50 beside the amount). A 28-40 pt info strip (savings, ETA, progress, scheduled slot) docks directly above the CTA (Uber, DoorDash, Instacart, Deliveroo, Wolt, Gojek). Primary actions are always at the bottom, never in the nav bar; nav-bar text actions (Save/Cancel) appear only on sheets or small forms.
9. **Bottom tab bar: 4-5 tabs, 49 pt + safe area, icon 24 + 10-11 pt label, active = brand colour + heavier icon.** 4 tabs: DoorDash, Gojek, TGTG, Instacart. 5: Wolt, HelloFresh, Swiggy. Uber Eats floats a pill instead. Gojek adds a 2 pt indicator line on top of the bar.
10. **Account / settings: plain icon rows, 43-55 pt pitch, not cards.** Uber 45, Gojek 55, Wolt 48, Instacart 43 (settings sheet), TGTG 47, HelloFresh 44 (inside grouped cream cards). Row = 20-24 pt icon + 16-17 pt label (+ optional 13 pt gray sub-line or right-aligned gray value) + chevron. Grouping by a 12-13 pt gray label (Gojek, TGTG "SETTINGS") or by a 20 pt bold label (Wolt, Instacart); groups are 20-24 pt apart. Uber and Instacart add a 3-tile shortcut row (108-111 pt wide x 64-70 pt tall, radius 12) above the list. Header is compact: avatar 56-72 pt, name 20-28 pt. 9-14 rows are visible in one screen. Log out / Delete account is the last row, plain text (red or default), not a big button.
11. **Dividers vs whitespace vs tone:** lists use a 1 px hairline inset 16 pt (menu rows, settings, activity). Sections are separated by an 8 pt gray band (Uber, DoorDash, Swiggy, Deliveroo) or 28-40 pt of whitespace. Containers differ by tone not border in HelloFresh (beige on cream), DoorDash Browse (raised gray tile) and Instacart Account. Soft cards use radius 12-16 and a 1 px border or a very light shadow, never both heavy.
12. **Checkout / summary:** fee rows 28-44 pt pitch at 14-16 pt, label left and value right-aligned, total 17-18 pt bold, strikethrough old price in gray and new price in a highlight colour. Sections have 19-20 pt headings with a "Change" teal/blue link at the right (Deliveroo, Wolt). Tip = 4-5 equal pill chips 34-39 pt tall. Payment row 56-72 pt bordered with "Change". Selected option = 2 pt dark border, radius 12 (Uber, DoorDash, Instacart). The page is one scroll with the CTA docked.
13. **State changes reuse the same slots.** Home pages keep one fixed slot for a live order status (Instacart status card at the top 65-118 pt, Swiggy and Zomato floating bar 47-60 pt above the tab bar, Deliveroo teal toast 56 pt, TGTG banner above the location row). A different state (confirmed, preparing, rate-your-order, canceled, skipped, closed) swaps the text, a pill or the button, not the structure. Same for HelloFresh "Skipped" (pink date tile + red "Unskip") and Wolt "Closed" (one blue schedule bar).
14. **Corner radius family:** cards and photos 12-16 (20 in Swiggy), chips and CTAs full pill or 8-12, inputs 8-12, sheets 20-28 on top, circles 36-44 for icon buttons on photos. Tap targets: icon buttons 36-44 pt, row 44-56 pt, steppers 32-42 pt tall, chips 32-36 pt, CTA 47-53 pt.
15. **First-viewport composition (home screens).** Header 44-56 pt, search 44 pt, a quick-nav row 62-100 pt (categories), then either a dominant hero or two stacked rails. Swiggy's stage is about 55% of the viewport, Grab's header + first rail about 63%, Wolt's hero is 24%, Uber's banner 14%. Information-dense apps (Uber, DoorDash, Wolt, Instacart) show 2-3 sections above the fold; photo-first apps (HelloFresh, TGTG) show one section plus a peek of the next.

### 3.2 Distinctive moves worth stealing (one app each)

1. **HelloFresh delivery-group block.** Date tile (about 70x57, stacked weekday / day / month) + 19 pt bold state ("Coming up") + 14 pt gray deadline ("Edit delivery by Saturday") + a strip of 175 pt meal tiles (2 visible, third peeks) + two stacked 47 pt buttons (filled primary, outlined secondary). Repeats at about 380 pt per block, so the next week is always half-visible. State differences are only a tile tint and one button. Add the "Manage your delivery" bottom sheet: 3 stacked outlined 61 pt actions, each with a 28 pt icon (reschedule, change size, donate) and a final "Skip this week" outlined button after a 13 pt prompt. And the week-picker date chips (78x55, selected lime) and the 50 pt calendar cell grid with a 32 pt selected circle.
2. **A fixed "live status" slot above the content or the tab bar** (Instacart top status card with an inline "Add items to order" green button; Swiggy floating 47 pt bar with ETA badge, which turns into a "rate your order" star row; Zomato "arriving in 3 mins" mini card). Home never grows a new section for a live order: one slot, whatever the state.
3. **Too Good To Go detail page skeleton.** Hero 219 pt, 35 pt info rows with icons (item type, rating, pickup window + date chip), address row, then description, then a rated breakdown, with one sticky bar: struck price over 20 pt bold price at the left, a 164x48 pill CTA at the right. Perforated "ticket" card on the list view. Fits a package detail (price + "Pilih paket" at the bottom).
4. **Instacart/Uber account hub:** 3 square shortcut tiles (Orders / Settings / Help) on top, then headings + plain rows with no dividers. Faster to read than a long uniform list.
5. **Wolt's "See all" pill (76x34, tinted fill)** as the trailing action on every heading, plus the venue page's logo overlapping a curved hero and a 3-control action row (selector + two 40 pt icon buttons). A docked "View order" bar that has a dark progress banner (a visible reward goal) attached on top.
6. **DoorDash Browse tiles:** 175x110 pt tiles, label top-left, food photo bleeding off the bottom-right. Image-forward navigation without icons.
7. **Swiggy stage header:** one saturated, rounded-bottom header holding tabs + search + a promo rail, then a white sheet. Makes the first screen feel composed rather than stacked. (Use sparingly: it consumes 55% of the viewport.)
8. **Gojek's inline right-column action:** price top-right and a 60x36 pill (Chat / Reorder) beneath it on every activity row; the order summary's two side-by-side docked buttons (outlined "Need help" + filled "Reorder").
9. **Deliveroo's tinted page + white cards** with heading rows that carry a "Change" link, and the "Round up / 2 / 5 / 10" donation chip row. Cards read clearly without borders because the page tone differs from the card tone.
10. **Uber Eats checkout "Looks good (0:02)" countdown confirm card** and the 4-segment tracking progress bar (4 pt tall, 6 pt gaps): a clean status display that works at one glance.
