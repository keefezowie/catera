# Uber Eats (iOS) patterns for Catera, 2026-10-30

Research only. Source: Mobbin, Uber Eats iOS, 16 calls (3 flow searches, 13 screen searches). Everything below was read off the screen images, not metadata. Catera evidence: `apps/customer/src`, `apps/caterer/src/today`, `DESIGN.md` (Loading, Brand artwork, Feedback), and screenshots in `output/native-review/` (`auth-2026-10-27/final/customer/01-masuk-phone-empty-light-id.png`, `deep-pages-gate-2026-10-24/r2-p3-beranda-light.png`, `deep-pages-gate-2026-10-24/r2-akun.png`, `deep-pages-2026-10-24/tabs/03-jelajah.png`, `layout-2026-10-24/gateC/hi-pre.png`).

The short version: Uber Eats is a plain app. White ground, black type, black pill buttons, grey rows. The personality comes from three things on top of that plain base:

1. **A different, specific picture for each moment.** Every empty state, primer and confirmation draws the object it is about (cart, bag, map route, calendar tote, exploded burger). None of them uses a mascot or a generic "nothing here" image.
2. **Real food, cut out and arranged.** Plates bleed off the screen edge, sit on wavy badges, or float as round tokens you can pick.
3. **Copy that is short, names the person or the thing, and says what is happening right now** ("Preparing your order...", "While you wait, Alex...", "Jack in the Box worked their magic for you").

Catera already has the plain base (white pages, forest pill, Plus Jakarta Sans) and good food photos. What it lacks is the first item: a specific picture per moment. It reuses five mascot poses everywhere instead, and it has no onboarding at all.

**Onboarding rules drawn from this:**
- One question per screen, a title phrased as a question, at most one reason line under it.
- Pictures go on screens that ask for trust (location, push, preferences). Plain form screens get none.
- Every ask has a no that still moves forward: "Skip", "Skip for now", "Do this later", "Enter address".
- The flow ends by showing your own answers back to you (step 8).
