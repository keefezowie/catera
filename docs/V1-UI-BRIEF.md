# Catera V1 surface brief

## Direction contract
THESIS: Recurring meals made tangible through the food arriving next and the calendar that connects every caterer.
OWN-WORLD: Approved forest, cream, sunrise and charcoal; Plus Jakarta Sans, regenerated rounded wordmark, generous food photography, calm operational tables.
STORY: Pick an address, understand a package, secure portions, and know exactly what arrives next.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Cream marketplace with address/search toolbar, a split forest editorial meal introduction, meal-category controls, and a generous three-column package grid. Subscriber home foregrounds the next meal and a lunch/dinner agenda. Seller home foregrounds the selected day's work.
FORM: User-approved food-led marketplace and delivery cycle, code-first; no concept selection is reopened.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

All prototype listings and commercial values are synthetic. Assets are newly generated; the reference board is not a runtime asset. Customer web/mobile have five destinations. Seller and platform admin have separate navigation and authorization.
