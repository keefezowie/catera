## Research and design rationale

Firecrawl research used three primary design references:

- Nielsen Norman Group, [Progressive Disclosure](https://www.nngroup.com/videos/progressive-disclosure/): keep essential customer information visible and reveal package/payment detail on request.
- Nielsen Norman Group, [Responsive Design](https://www.nngroup.com/topic/responsive-design/): adapt dense operational information to the viewport rather than preserving a desktop card grid on phones.
- Carbon Design System, [Forms pattern](https://preview.carbondesignsystem.com/building-blocks/core/patterns/forms): visibly group and align labels, fields, and related actions.

Impeccable guided the final frontend pass. Its one detector run reported advisory-only design-token drift. The new customer type sizes were aligned to the documented Catera ramp after that review; other reported literal sizes, radii, and colors are pre-existing declarations in the shared usability and settlement stylesheets and were not broadened into an unrelated redesign.
